Frequently Asked Questions

Is Luck-O free to use?

Yes. Luck-O is completely free with no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Can I win real money with Luck-O?

No. Luck-O is for entertainment purposes only. It does not sell lottery tickets or involve real-money gambling of any kind.

Is Luck-O affiliated with any official lottery?

No. Luck-O is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or associated with any official lottery organization.

Does Luck-O collect my personal data?

No. We do not collect any personal data. Your privacy is fully respected.

What devices are supported?

Luck-O is available on iPhone and requires iOS 26.0 or later.

The app is crashing or not working properly.

Please make sure you are running the latest version from the App Store. If the issue persists, contact us using the email below and include your device model and iOS version.
